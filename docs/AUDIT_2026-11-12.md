standard and embodied mode, with the authors' 71 paths, extra counterexamples and a Mandarin matcher test. This page
lists each finding, what changed, and the test that now holds it. Every case moves to version 0.2.0.

## Not done, and why

- **A genuinely held-out language set.** The audit's questions are now regression tests: they have been seen, so
  they are no longer held out. A held-out set must be written and clinically annotated by someone who has not seen
  the keywords, and kept out of the corpus.
- **Device-level pharmacy and acupuncture tasks; tongue images; pattern reasoning beyond a first-try choice; active
  teamwork; patients' responses generated from what was dispensed.** These need engine work:
  docs/ENGINE_EXTENSIONS.md §1, §2, §4, §6 and §7, with the audit's findings added there.
- **Clinical sign-off.** All seven cases remain `unreviewed` and are not for teaching use until a registered TCM
  physician (and a family physician where conventional care is involved) has reviewed them.
- **Held-out cases for model evaluation.** All three families are in the dev split. The test split is the five
  CMCR adaptations (docs/PHASE2.md), which still wait on the permission basis for the source reports.
